# Audio Overviews

NotebookLM can generate podcast-style discussions about your sources. Two AI hosts talk through the key points like it's an episode of a research podcast.

---

## What You Get

A 5-15 minute audio file where two voices:

- Summarize major themes from your sources
- Highlight interesting findings
- Discuss implications conversationally
- Cover key points in an engaging format

It's surprisingly listenable. Not groundbreaking journalism, but solid for absorbing information while commuting, exercising, or doing dishes.

---

## How to Generate One

1. Open a notebook with sources already added
2. Click **"Generate Audio Overview"** in the Notebook Guide panel
3. (Optional) Add focus instructions like "Focus on risk factors and competitive positioning"
4. Wait a few minutes for generation
5. Play or download the audio

---

## Good Use Cases

**10-K orientation.** Generate an audio overview before deep diving. Gives you the lay of the land.

**Earnings call digests.** Upload a transcript, get an audio summary you can listen to instead of reading.

**Comparison primers.** With multiple competitors' filings, get a conversational comparison of their strategies.

**Memory refresh.** Revisit old research without re-reading. The audio hits the highlights.

---

## Customization Tips

Use the focus instruction to steer the discussion:

```
Focus on management's outlook for next year and any guidance changes.
```

```
Emphasize competitive threats and how the company responds to them.
```

```
Cover the risk factors section in depth.
```

Without instructions, you get a general overview. With instructions, you get targeted discussion.

---

## Interactive Mode (Newer Feature)

Some versions of NotebookLM Plus let you "join" the audio as it plays and ask follow-up questions. The hosts respond in real-time.

This is cool but not essential. The base audio overviews are valuable on their own.

---

## Limitations

**Not a replacement for reading.** Audio overviews are summaries. They skip details that might matter.

**Can't verify while listening.** You don't get clickable citations in audio. Use it for orientation, not final analysis.

**Generation limits.** Free tier has limited audio generations per period. Plus tier gets more.

**Quality varies.** Some overviews are great. Some are superficial. Depends on your sources and luck.

---

## Best Practices

1. **Generate after adding all relevant sources** — The overview draws from everything in the notebook
2. **Add focus instructions** — Steer toward what you care about
3. **Use for first pass, not final pass** — Get oriented, then do real analysis in the chat
4. **Download useful ones** — They're just audio files you can save
