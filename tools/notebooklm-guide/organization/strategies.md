# Notebook Strategies

How you organize notebooks affects how useful they are. Here are patterns that work.

---

## Two Main Approaches

### Company-Centric

**One notebook per company** with everything about that company inside.

```
AAPL Research/
├── AAPL 10-K FY2024
├── AAPL 10-K FY2023
├── AAPL Q1 2025 Earnings Transcript
├── AAPL Q4 2024 Earnings Transcript
└── AAPL Investor Day Presentation
```

**Good for:**
- Deep dives on individual companies
- Tracking one company over time
- Understanding a single business thoroughly

**Queries enabled:**
- Year-over-year changes in risk factors
- How management narrative evolved
- Longitudinal strategic tracking

---

### Sector/Competitive

**One notebook per comparison group** with multiple companies.

```
Semiconductor Comparison/
├── NVDA 10-K FY2024
├── AMD 10-K FY2024
├── INTC 10-K FY2024
└── QCOM 10-K FY2024
```

**Good for:**
- Comparative analysis
- Understanding competitive dynamics
- Sector research

**Queries enabled:**
- Cross-company risk comparison
- Competitive positioning differences
- Shared vs. unique challenges

---

## When to Split Notebooks

Split when:
- You hit the 50-source limit
- Queries are pulling irrelevant sources
- Topics are genuinely unrelated
- You want focused audio overviews

Keep together when:
- Sources relate to the same question
- You want cross-source comparison
- Everything fits within limits

---

## Hybrid Approach

For serious ongoing research, consider both:

**Company notebooks** for deep individual coverage:
- `NVDA Deep Dive` (all NVDA materials)
- `AMD Deep Dive` (all AMD materials)

**Comparison notebooks** for competitive views:
- `GPU Sector Comparison` (latest 10-Ks from all players)

Yes, this means some duplication. Worth it for different query types.

---

## Project-Based Notebooks

For specific research projects:

```
AI Infrastructure Thesis/
├── NVDA 10-K (latest)
├── AMD 10-K (latest)  
├── SMCI 10-K (latest)
├── Industry Report on AI Compute
└── Recent earnings calls (key ones)
```

Focused on a specific investment question, not comprehensive coverage.

---

## What Not to Do

**Don't create one giant notebook** with everything you've ever researched. Queries become unfocused, and you hit limits fast.

**Don't create a notebook per document.** You lose the multi-source comparison power.

**Don't mix unrelated companies** unless you're specifically comparing them. Random noise in answers.

---

## Maintenance

**Update regularly.** When new filings come out, add them.

**Prune old sources.** If you don't need 5 years of history, remove older filings to reduce noise.

**Archive completed research.** If you're done analyzing a company, leave the notebook but don't add to it.
