# NotebookLM for Investors

<span class="last-updated">Last updated: January 2025</span>

A no-BS guide to using Google's NotebookLM for investment research.

---

## What You'll Learn

NotebookLM is Google's AI research tool that reads your documents and answers questions about them — with citations you can actually verify. It's genuinely useful for investment research, but it has real limitations you need to understand.

This guide covers:

- **Getting Started** — Set up your first research notebook in 5 minutes
- **Core Skills** — How to add sources, ask questions, and verify answers
- **Investment Workflows** — Practical walkthroughs for 10-Ks, earnings calls, and competitive analysis
- **Prompt Playbook** — Copy-paste prompts that actually work
- **Know the Limits** — What NotebookLM can't do (this matters more than you think)

---

## The Bottom Line

**NotebookLM is great for:**
- Summarizing long documents (10-Ks, annual reports, earnings transcripts)
- Finding specific information across multiple sources
- Comparing what different companies say about the same topic
- Getting citations you can click and verify

**NotebookLM is NOT for:**
- Pulling exact numbers from financial statements (it makes mistakes)
- Any calculations (it literally can't do math)
- Reading charts or graphs (it can't see them)
- Real-time data (no internet access)

> If you take one thing from this guide: **always verify financial numbers against the original source.** NotebookLM is a research accelerator, not a replacement for your eyes on the actual filings.
