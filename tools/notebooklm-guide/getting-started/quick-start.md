# Quick Start

Get up and running in 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Go to NotebookLM

Visit [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com) and sign in with your Google account.

---

## Step 2: Create a Notebook

Click **"New Notebook"** in the top left.

Think of a notebook as a research project. One notebook = one topic. For investing, this might be:
- A single company you're researching
- A sector comparison
- A specific investment thesis

---

## Step 3: Add a Source

Click **"Add Source"** and pick your document type:

- **PDF** — 10-Ks, annual reports, research PDFs
- **Google Docs** — Notes, transcripts you've copied
- **Website** — Investor relations pages, articles
- **YouTube** — Earnings calls with video (it extracts the transcript)
- **Google Drive** — Files already in your Drive

For your first test, try uploading a 10-K PDF from a company you're interested in. Get these from [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/searchedgar/companysearch).

---

## Step 4: Wait for Processing

NotebookLM needs a minute to read and index your document. You'll see a progress indicator.

For a typical 10-K (100-200 pages), this takes 30-60 seconds.

---

## Step 5: Start Asking Questions

Once processing completes, you'll see a chat interface. Try these starter questions:

```
What does this company do?
```

```
What are the main risk factors?
```

```
Summarize the management discussion and analysis section.
```

Notice how each answer includes numbered citations like [1], [2]. Click them to see exactly where that information came from.

---

## Step 6: Check the Notebook Guide

On the right side, you'll see automatically generated content:
- A summary of your sources
- Suggested questions
- Key topics

This is helpful for getting oriented with an unfamiliar document.

---

That's the basics. You now know enough to start researching.
