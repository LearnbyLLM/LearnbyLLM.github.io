# What is NotebookLM?

NotebookLM is Google's AI-powered research assistant. You upload documents, and it answers questions about them — citing exactly where the information came from.

---

## How It's Different from ChatGPT

| | ChatGPT | NotebookLM |
|---|---------|------------|
| **Knowledge source** | Trained on the internet | Only your uploaded documents |
| **Citations** | Sometimes, inconsistent | Always, clickable to source |
| **Hallucinations** | Common | Reduced (but not zero) |
| **Real-time info** | With plugins/browsing | None |
| **Best for** | General questions, writing | Analyzing specific documents |

The key difference: ChatGPT draws from everything it was trained on. NotebookLM only knows what you give it. This is a feature, not a bug — it means answers are grounded in your sources, and you can verify every claim.

---

## Core Concept: Grounded AI

When you ask NotebookLM a question, it:

1. Searches your uploaded sources
2. Finds relevant passages
3. Synthesizes an answer
4. Provides clickable citations

If the answer isn't in your documents, it tells you "I don't have information about that" instead of making something up.

This makes it particularly useful for investment research where accuracy matters.

---

## What It Runs On

NotebookLM uses Google's **Gemini 1.5 Pro** model under the hood. You don't need to know the technical details, but it's worth knowing this is a capable, current-generation AI — not some stripped-down version.

---

## Free vs. Paid

**Free tier:** Solid for most individual research. Enough sources and queries for typical use.

**NotebookLM Plus (~$20/month via Google One AI Premium):**
- More sources per notebook
- More Audio Overview generations
- Priority access to new features

For most people in an investing group chat, the free tier is fine to start. Upgrade if you hit limits.

---

## What You Need

- A Google account
