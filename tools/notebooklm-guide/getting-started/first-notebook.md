# Your First Research Notebook

Let's build a real investment research notebook step by step.

---

## The Scenario

You want to understand Apple's business and risks before deciding whether to invest. We'll create a notebook, add their most recent 10-K, and extract useful insights.

---

## Get the 10-K

1. Go to [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=apple&type=10-K)
2. Find Apple Inc. (AAPL)
3. Click on the most recent 10-K filing
4. Download the complete submission as PDF

Or just Google "Apple 10-K 2024 PDF" — investor relations pages usually have direct downloads.

---

## Create the Notebook

1. Go to [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com)
2. Click **New Notebook**
3. Name it something useful: `AAPL Research` or `Apple 2024 Analysis`

---

## Add the 10-K

1. Click **Add Source**
2. Select **Upload** → Choose your PDF
3. Wait for processing (about a minute for a 10-K)

---

## Explore with the Notebook Guide

Before asking questions, look at the auto-generated Notebook Guide on the right. It shows:

- **Summary** — A quick overview of what's in the document
- **Key Topics** — Main themes NotebookLM identified
- **Suggested Questions** — Good starting points

This gives you a feel for what's in the document without reading 100+ pages.

---

## Ask Research Questions

Start broad, then go specific. Here's a progression that works:

### Understanding the Business

```
What are Apple's main business segments and what percentage of revenue does each contribute?
```

```
How does Apple describe its competitive advantages?
```

### Risk Assessment

```
List the main risk factors, grouped by category.
```

```
What does management say about supply chain concentration?
```

### Forward-Looking View

```
What forward-looking statements does management make about growth?
```

```
What capital expenditure plans are mentioned?
```

---

## Verify the Numbers

Let's say NotebookLM tells you "Services revenue was $85 billion, representing 22% of total revenue."

**Always do this:**
1. Click the citation number
2. Find that passage in the original PDF
3. Confirm the numbers match

NotebookLM often gets numbers approximately right but sometimes misreads tables or mixes up periods. Don't trust financial figures without verification.

---

## Save Useful Answers

NotebookLM doesn't have a built-in notes feature, so:

- Copy good answers into a Google Doc or Notion
- Screenshot key findings
- Or just keep the chat history (it persists in the notebook)

---

## What You Should Have Learned

After this exercise, you should understand:

- Apple's main revenue sources
- Key risks management has disclosed
- Major strategic themes
- Where to dig deeper

All without reading the entire 10-K. That's the value — NotebookLM compresses hours of reading into minutes of focused Q&A.
